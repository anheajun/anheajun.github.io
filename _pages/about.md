I am a third-year Ph.D. student in the [Department of Computer Science](https://cs.vt.edu/) at [Virginia Tech](https://www.vt.edu/), advised by Professor [Jin-Hee Cho](https://website.cs.vt.edu/people/faculty/jin-hee-cho.html). I am a member of the [Trustworthy Cyberspace Lab (tClab)](https://people.cs.vt.edu/~jicho/). My research lies at the intersection of AI safety, cybersecurity, and human-centered AI, with a particular interest in building safer and more trustworthy AI systems for vulnerable users. I study how AI systems can fail or mislead users in safety-critical settings, and how we can better evaluate, mitigate, and recover from these failures.

## Research Interests

**AI Safety and Guardrails**
Developing methods to evaluate and mitigate unsafe, harmful, or misleading behaviors in large language models, including modular and audience-specific guardrails.

**Human-Centered AI Safety**
Studying how people interpret, trust, and interact with AI systems, with a particular interest in designing safer and more trustworthy AI for vulnerable users.

**AI for Online Safety and Security**
Developing AI-driven approaches to protect users from online harms, including scams, cybergrooming, and other forms of deceptive or harmful interactions.
